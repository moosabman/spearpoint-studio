FAQ

Good questions.

How long does a training video take?

About 2–3 weeks from kickoff for a single video. A series or library runs on a schedule we agree on in your quote.

Do you use AI?

Yes, for parts of production like visuals and editing, under a written policy. We only use business versions of tools whose terms say your inputs aren’t used to train their models, and employee personal details, customer data and financials never go into any AI tool. Read the policy.

How do you keep our information confidential?

We sign a mutual NDA before you send anything, keep your files in a locked-down folder only the two of us can open, and delete raw materials 90 days after delivery, or sooner if you ask.

Who owns the finished videos?

You do, once they’re paid for. We don’t show internal training in our portfolio without your written permission.

What do you need from us?

Your SOPs, slides or manuals, an hour with the person who knows the process best, and one person who approves the script and the cuts.

Can you film on site or make Spanish versions?

Yes. An on-site filming day is $1,200, and a Spanish version of a video is $400.

How does payment work?

50% to start and 50% on delivery. Monthly plans are billed at the start of each month, with a 3-month minimum.

Where are you?

Austin, Texas. We’re happy to meet in person around Austin and work with teams anywhere.

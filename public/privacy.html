Privacy & AI policy

How we handle your information.

Training material holds a company’s processes, its policies and sometimes its people. These are the rules we follow on every project, and we put them in writing in every agreement.

Last updated November 2, 2026

1. NDA before anything is shared

We sign a mutual non-disclosure agreement before you send us any materials. Nothing confidential changes hands until it’s signed.

2. How we use AI

We use AI tools for parts of production, such as visuals, editing and voiceover. When we do:

  • We only use business versions of AI tools whose terms say client inputs aren’t used to train their models, and we check each tool’s terms before using it on client work.
  • We never put employee personal details, customer data or financial information into any AI tool.

3. No faces or voices without consent

We never create an AI likeness or AI voice of a real employee without that person’s written permission. You approve any AI voice or AI presenter before it appears in your video.

4. Private storage, then deletion

  • Each client gets one locked-down shared folder.
  • Every account we use has two-step login turned on.
  • Only Jordan and Kevin have access.
  • Raw materials you send us are deleted 90 days after delivery, or sooner if you ask.

5. You own the finished work

Once a project is paid for, you own the final videos. For internal training, we never show any of your work in our portfolio or anywhere else without your written permission. The default is no.

6. Accuracy stays with you

Your subject-matter expert signs off on every script before production starts. Content about policy, safety or legal requirements should be reviewed by your HR or legal team.

Questions about this policy? Email hello@spearpointpro.com and Jordan or Kevin will answer directly.
